Giveaway Tools runs entirely in your browser. The roller, Guess the Balance and slot calls read your Kick chat directly, and slot RTP, volatility, max win and release figures come from the free slot database at slot.report, which tracks thousands of games from dozens of providers and updates daily.

How the roll stays fair

Random push. The launch speed and the starting slot both come from crypto.getRandomValues, the browser's cryptographic random source. Nothing about the result is chosen in advance.

Real physics. The reel is simulated at 480 steps per second with air drag and rolling friction, and nothing nudges it toward a name. It stops wherever it stops, and the line picks the name it's touching. The fixed time step means a slow device changes how smooth it looks, never where it lands.

No order bias. Entries are reshuffled before every roll and the reel starts on a uniformly random slot, so join order or first letter can't tilt the odds. Each person gets one slot however often they type. With subscriber luck, subscribers get a second slot, placed randomly and kept apart from their first whenever possible.
